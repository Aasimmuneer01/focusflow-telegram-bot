FocusFlow Telegram Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. About the Bot

FocusFlow Bot is a study and productivity assistant designed to help users organize their learning activities.

2. Information Processing

Telegram sends messages and basic account or chat information when you interact with the bot. This information is processed as needed to respond to commands.

3. Storage and Use

The current bot version does not intentionally save study tasks, notes, or reminders in a personal database. Telegram and the hosting provider may process technical data under their own policies.

4. Sharing

We do not intentionally sell personal information. Telegram and the bot's hosting service process information as needed to operate the bot.

5. Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect bot credentials and configuration, but absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

6. Children's Privacy

Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information to the bot. Children should use online services with appropriate guidance from a parent or guardian.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the bot's features change. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the bot owner through the FocusFlow bot's Telegram support option.
