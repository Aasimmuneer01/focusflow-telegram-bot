FocusFlow Telegram Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. About the Bot

FocusFlow Bot is a study and productivity assistant designed to help users organize their learning activities.

2. Information Processing

When you interact with the bot, Telegram sends your messages and basic account or chat information needed to deliver the interaction. The bot processes incoming messages to respond to commands and requests.

3. Storage and Use

The current bot version is designed to respond to commands and does not intentionally save study tasks, notes, or reminders in a personal database. Hosting providers and Telegram may process technical data under their own policies. This may change if new features are added.

4. Sharing

We do not intentionally sell your personal information. Messages are processed through Telegram and the bot's hosting service as needed to operate the bot.

5. Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect bot credentials and configuration. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

If you are a child, use the bot with guidance from a parent or guardian where appropriate. Please do not send passwords, sensitive personal details, or private information to the bot.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the bot's features change. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact the FocusFlow bot owner through the bot's Telegram support option.
